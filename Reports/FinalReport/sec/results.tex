\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Best landmark-detector result: validation MAE = 4.
Best face-cropper result: validation bbox MAE = 0.05.
These MAE values refer to the validation set.

Figure~\ref{fig:landmark_detector_results} shows qualitative predictions of the self-trained landmark detector on 9 samples (3$\times$3 grid), including predicted outputs and labels.
In this panel, landmark localization is visibly successful for most samples.
Some samples are only moderately cropped or landmarked.
For \textit{Sample 36}, the facial landmarks are not detected.
