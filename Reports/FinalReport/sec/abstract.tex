\begin{abstract}
This report presents our general strategy for six-class facial emotion recognition under practical constraints.
Because compute resources are limited and several datasets are small or heterogeneous, we rely on compact models instead of large architectures.
The core idea is to improve input quality first through structured data preparation and strong augmentation.
This reduces instability and helps the model learn clearer patterns from limited data.

\end{abstract}
