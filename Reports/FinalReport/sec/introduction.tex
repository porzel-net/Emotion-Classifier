\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{Context and relevance}
Facial emotion recognition is important for human-centered AI systems, but practical deployments are often constrained by limited compute and low-resolution inputs.
In this project, we therefore focus on a compact and resource-aware setup.

\subsection{Problem statement}
Our main challenge is to classify six emotions from limited and heterogeneous data while keeping the model small.
Small datasets and domain differences increase overfitting risk, and weak input signals make feature learning unstable.

\subsection{Goal and research question}
The goal is to reach high classification quality despite limited resources.
Our central research question is: \textit{How can strong data preparation and augmentation enable high accuracy with small models under constrained resources?}
